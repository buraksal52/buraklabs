## Research question

Interface quality is often discussed through subjective review. LucidUI explores whether design principles and measurable criteria can support a more systematic evaluation process.

## Method direction

The project combines a Python analysis layer with a React interface, OCR/Tesseract-based extraction and a rule-based scoring mechanism. It investigates analysis of UI design properties and an AI-assisted recommendation layer.

Comparative exploration with AI-based approaches such as UIClip is part of the research direction, not a claim of completed or validated benchmark results.

## Academic context

The work is described as research-oriented and was conducted under the supervision of Dr. Didem Tufan. An E-HCI 2026 publication target was discussed; no publication, acceptance or submission is claimed here.
