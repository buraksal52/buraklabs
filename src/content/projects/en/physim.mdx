## Problem

Exam-preparation tools do not always model what a student’s mistakes reveal about specific concepts. Counting correct and incorrect answers alone is not enough to explain why a learner is struggling.

## Learning-system direction

Physim explores tracking learning outcomes individually, modeling misconceptions and representing mastery at the outcome level. A spaced-repetition approach can use that learning history to plan review around weaker areas.

The central idea is to treat an incorrect answer as a signal about a concept or learning outcome, rather than only as a failure count.

## Engineering and pedagogy

The project focuses on translating pedagogical decisions into software models: learner state, mastery updates, misconception signals and personalized review planning.

## Evidence boundary

No scientifically verified improvement in learning outcomes is claimed. The implementation status of each planned feature, experiments, users, metrics and deployment requires owner verification.
