## Problem

Blockchain payments usually assume a reliable network connection. Ghostellar explored the payment experience in places where connectivity is unavailable or intermittent.

## Approach

The prototype used QR codes to share payment information while offline. Pending operations could be held in a temporary pool and considered for online verification and blockchain processing once connectivity returned.

The design also considered transaction timestamps, metadata, pending-operation prioritization, transaction ordering and double-spending risks.

## Engineering decisions

- Treat offline operations as pending intent rather than as already-finalized blockchain transactions.
- Keep synchronization and verification separate from the offline exchange step.
- Make security limitations explicit in the product model.

## Context and outcome

Ghostellar was developed during the Stellar Pro Hackathon in an approximately 27-hour working period. The project reached the final round among approximately 80 teams, where six teams presented.

These statements describe the hackathon context and prototype result. They do not mean that the prototype solved all security concerns or finalized transactions on-chain without an internet connection.
