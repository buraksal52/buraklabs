## 2. About

### English

**Who I am:**

I'm Burak, a Computer Engineering student at Ankara Bilim University and an independent developer interested in the intersection of software engineering, artificial intelligence, computer systems, and human-computer interaction.

I approach engineering through curiosity, experimentation, and hands-on development. I enjoy understanding how systems work beneath the surface, from operating systems and infrastructure to intelligent applications and user-facing products.

My interests span multiple disciplines, but they share a common goal: understanding complex technologies deeply enough to turn them into useful, reliable systems.

**What I do:**

I design, develop, and experiment with software products and research-driven prototypes.

My work includes AI-powered applications, LLM-based systems, backend infrastructure, developer tools, blockchain applications, adaptive learning technologies, and computational approaches to human-computer interaction.

I primarily work with Python, Go, FastAPI, and Flutter, while continuously exploring lower-level systems, Linux infrastructure, machine learning, and distributed computing.

Through BurakLabs, I organize my work around three complementary directions:

- **Engineering:** Building reliable software, infrastructure, developer tools, and intelligent systems.
- **Products:** Transforming technical ideas into applications that solve practical problems.
- **Research:** Exploring measurable problems, evaluating hypotheses, and developing experimental systems.

**Where this leads:**

My long-term direction is to work at the intersection of research, advanced engineering, and entrepreneurship.

I want to contribute to meaningful technical research, develop original systems, and transform selected research outcomes into practical products.

BurakLabs serves as my independent engineering and research space: a place to document experiments, share technical work, develop products, and explore ideas that may eventually become larger research or business initiatives.

Yazılım ürünleri, araştırma odaklı prototipler ve deneysel sistemler tasarlıyor ve geliştiriyorum.

Ağırlıklı olarak Python, Go, FastAPI ve Flutter kullanıyorum. Bunun yanında Linux, sistem programlama, makine öğrenmesi ve dağıtık sistemler üzerine teknik yetkinliğimi geliştiriyorum.

BurakLabs kapsamındaki çalışmalarımı üç ana başlıkta topluyorum:

Özgün sistemler geliştirmek, bilimsel araştırmalara katkı sağlamak ve uygun araştırma çıktılarını gerçek dünyada kullanılabilen ürünlere dönüştürmek istiyorum.

BurakLabs bu yolculuğun bağımsız mühendislik ve araştırma alanı. Projelerimi, teknik deneylerimi, geliştirdiğim ürünleri ve araştırma çalışmalarımı tek bir çatı altında bir araya getiriyorum.
